# fig_hn_m_epoch_dynamics_panel

1. This asset answers: How the main formal gate metrics evolve across epochs for representative HN ratios.
2. Source files:
   - research/materials/stage1_formal/gate_hn_m_sweep/hn00/epoch_gate_summary.csv
   - research/materials/stage1_formal/gate_hn_m_sweep/hn02/epoch_gate_summary.csv
   - research/materials/stage1_formal/gate_hn_m_sweep/hn12/epoch_gate_summary.csv
3. Ranking/selection rule: Representative ratios are selected as hn00, the formal winner, and a high-ratio tail condition.
4. Key finding: The representative-ratio panel summarizes how HN affects the training dynamics beyond the final best-epoch snapshot.
5. Limitation: The panel is limited to representative ratios and does not replace the full ratio-level summaries.
